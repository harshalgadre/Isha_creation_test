— GOOD TO KNOW —

Little questions

How long does an order take?+

Each piece is made by hand, so it usually takes a few days depending on the item. We’ll confirm the exact time on WhatsApp before you pay.

Can I choose colours or add a name?+

Yes! Pick a colour on the product page, or message us for a custom colour combo, name or initial.

How do I pay?+

After you message us, we share the payment details on chat (UPI etc.). We start crocheting once it’s confirmed.

How do I care for crochet items?+

Keep away from water and excessive heat. For soft toys and baby wear, gentle hand wash and air dry.
